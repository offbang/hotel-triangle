About Hotel Triangle

A premier destination in the heart of Mbarara

Hotel Triangle blends contemporary design with genuine Ugandan warmth. Whether you are in town for business, a weekend getaway, a wedding or a corporate event, our team delivers service that feels personal and polished.

Unwind by the pool, dine in our restaurant and bar, host meetings in our conference spaces, and rest in rooms designed for comfort — all minutes from Mbarara's key destinations.
